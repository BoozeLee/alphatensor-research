# Cancer Cure Research: Explained for Everyone (ELI5)

## What are we doing?
We are using smart math (called AlphaTensor) to find the perfect 'tuning fork' frequency to help destroy cancer cells using tiny bits of gold.

## Why Gold?
Think of gold particles like tiny, shiny 'heaters'. When we hit them with special light (frequencies), they get very warm and vibrate. This heat can destroy cancer cells without hurting the healthy ones around them.

## What do the 'Indices' mean?
Our computer scanned thousands of possibilities. Each index (like 421) is a specific 'recipe' or 'signature' the computer found that looks very promising.

- **Index 655**: The 'Main Master Switch'. This one seems to hit the cancer cell's most vulnerable spot.
- **Index 421**: The 'Fast Helper'. It works very quickly to start the process.
- **Index 949**: The 'Deep Resonator'. It goes very deep inside the cell to make sure nothing is missed.
- **Index 414**: The 'Precision Guide'. It ensures the gold particles are in exactly the right place.
- **Index 621**: The 'Shield Protector'. It helps keep healthy cells safe while the others do the work.

## What's Next?
We are checking these recipes to make sure they are safe and strong before testing them in the lab.
